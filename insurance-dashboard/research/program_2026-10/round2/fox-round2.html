Bottom line

fox moved from 5.8 to 7.3 in three days. The fact base, provenance, exports and phone basics are now best in class for this niche. What stands between fox and 9.0 is no longer data or features. Nobody can find or quote it, it has news four times a year while analysts work daily, it looks like a well-kept admin panel, its operations are not yet trustworthy at speed, and the newest layers have coverage holes.

Where it should go: an analyst that reads every filing first, tells you what changed, lets you test what-if, and travels into other people's models and articles. All of it on free, public data.

Root causes

Five things hold fox below 9

Hidden

Every path sends noindex; every URL returns the same 1.8 KB shell titled "fox"; embeds are blocked. Google sends "Harel financials" to stockanalysis and TipRanks.

Quarterly product, daily job

Autopilot built but off. data.gov.il already has 09/26; fox shows 08/26 and calls it fresh. No alerts, sync off.

Admin-panel look

No lead on any screen, 66 controls before the first table on a company page, 17 font sizes against an 8-step scale, colour on levels instead of changes, charts without a frame.

Trust at speed

CI has failed on every push since 23.10 and the deploy ignores it. Four live data files are uncommitted. Company pages crash in WebKit. A missing chunk can be cached as HTML for a year.

Coverage holes

Solvency current for 2 of 7 insurers, ownership 1 of 14, holdings 5 of ≈20, 6,501 Phoenix cells cite links that now redirect to an error.

Five fixes that protect what is already live

P0WebKit crash on company pages. A destructuring loop in lib/company.ts throws on cached data (6 of 9 reloads of /company/phoenix in WebKit). Indexed loops, a WebKit phone project in CI, then a check on the owner's iPhone.
P0CI as the only door to live. Fix the autopilot test fixture, one Python version, deploy only from green CI (needs a Firebase service-account secret), commit generated data before every deploy.
P1Cache contract. Rewrite only extensionless paths, no-cache on HTML, real 404s for missing assets and data, reload on a failed chunk.
P1Gate every published layer. Solvency, adjusted KPIs, targets and decks checked on page at deploy; freshness measured against the upstream source; an honest coverage matrix (≈70%, not 95%).
P1Re-source the Phoenix cells to the MAYA filing, and run a weekly link check over every source.

Go public.Drop noindex. The series pages, embeds, share cards and every growth loop depend on it.

Who fox is for.Rewrite PRODUCT.md from "one user" to four segments: insurer and LTS analysts, industry insiders, journalists, allocators. A consumer tool only as a separate sub-brand.
