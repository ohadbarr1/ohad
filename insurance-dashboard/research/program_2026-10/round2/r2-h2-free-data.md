# r2-h2 · Free data scout — sources fox does not use yet (25 Oct 2026)

Scope: free/public sources that could power new modules for insurers, pension/gemel/managers' insurance and fund houses. Each row probed 25 Oct 2026 with single polite requests (no bot evasion). Tags: [V] verified live today, [T] second-hand (search result or press), [J] judgment. Already used and excluded: גמל-נט / פנסיה-נט / ביטוח-נט, MAYA reports + XBRL, BoI SDMX `BR`/`PRI`/`ZCM`, the CBS CPI, TASE end-of-day prices (valuation input only), and managers' quarterly asset-list XLSX files (`pipeline/fetch_*.py`, `asset_lists/`).

## Bottom line

One source changes the game: **the CMA's own public statement database, `insurancedata.cma.gov.il`** [V]. It holds regulator-filed financial statements and quarterly line-of-business management reports for about 30 insurance entities from 2002, for managing companies from 2011 and for pension and gemel funds from 2012, with IFRS 17 report families and years up to 2026 in its menus. It is not referenced anywhere in the repo or in round-1 research [V grep]. It would take fox from 8 listed insurers to the whole licensed universe (Libra, Wesure, Shirbit, AIG, Shlomo, Bituach Yashir, David Shield, Clal Credit, Karnit, the natural-disaster fund…), with industry totals and market share by line on a 24-year quarterly base. Nobody free has this; prodata has none of it. Next in value: **EIOPA Solvency II statistics** (an international solvency benchmark, CSV), **BoI `MF`/`INSINV2`** (the fund-house market tide, on SDMX code fox already has), the **CMA complaints report + service index** (a conduct scorecard), and **Knesset OData + CMA regulation plan** (a regulatory radar). Dead routes (stop proposing them) are listed under the table.

## Notes

**1. How to build #1 without breaking trust.** Treat it as a new *layer*, never a substitute: label every figure "דוח סולו לרשות" with query parameters as provenance (domain, report, period, entity), and keep it out of the consolidated statement explorer. First cut, ≈2–3 sessions [J]: (a) management reports (premiums, profit, liabilities by line × company × quarter, 2002→) → a market-share-by-line page and an industry-total column in the Peer Table Builder; (b) managing-company P&L for all gemel/pension managers → the Savings Lens covers the long tail (Infinity, Mor gemel, Altshuler gemel solo…); (c) unlisted insurers' solo P&L and balance sheet → "the whole market" rankings. Scripting: replay the form with a cookie jar, one query every 2–3 s, cache raw Excel exports under `data/raw/cma_db/` with the request hash, then diff on refresh (the regulator restates). Ask the CMA in writing for permission or a bulk file; it de-risks the dependency [J].

**2. Access-risk flags fox should act on now.** (a) `maya.tase.co.il/robots.txt` says `Disallow: /api/` and that "bulk extraction … require[s] explicit authorization"; `market.tase.co.il` carries the same note [V]. fox's `fetch_maya.py`, `fetch_xbrl.py` and `fetch_prices.py` use exactly those endpoints. Low volume probably keeps this tolerable, but it is the single largest platform risk: write to TASE for authorisation and keep the MAYA PDFs cached as the system of record. (b) `data.gov.il/robots.txt` also disallows `/api/` [V], though the CKAN API is the documented access path under an open licence: keep request rates low and log fetches (fox already does). (c) Every gov.il **HTML** page returns a Cloudflare challenge to scripts, while BlobFolder PDFs do not [V]. Don't try to pass the challenge. Keep a curated `sources.yaml` of annual PDF URLs, refreshed by hand once a year, so the gov.il sources (#4, #6, #10, plans, circulars) work without automation.

**3. Cylinders.** Data gains two independent checks (regulator solo totals against group statements; BoI `INSINV2` against fox's regulator AUM). Strategy gains "every licensed insurer and manager, 24 years deep, benchmarked to Europe and the OECD, free". Workflows gain three fundamentals-only modules (radar, conduct, ratings). UX cylinders need only one new pattern: an industry-total / international-quartile band on existing charts.

**4. Sequencing.** Phase A (≤1 week): #2, #3, #7, #11, #12 are all small API/CSV jobs on existing fetch patterns. Phase B (2–4 weeks): #1 (management reports, then managing companies, then unlisted insurers) and #8. Phase C (quarter): #4 + #10 as the conduct scorecard, #5 regulatory radar, #6 line book, #9 class-action tracker. Vision-extracted values (#4, #10) need the same value-on-page gate as `ifrs_*.json` before they ship.

**5. Sparring note.** "Court rulings vs insurers" and "clearing-house flows" fail on access, not value: build class-action exposure from the litigation notes fox already holds (#9), and per-manager switching from the `INTERNAL_TRANSFERS` field fox already ingests from גמל-נט/פנסיה-נט.
