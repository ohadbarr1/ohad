# r2-h1 · Horizons: where fox can go next (25 Oct 2026)

Lens: new directions, not polish. Tags: [V] verified today in the repo or with a live call, [T] second-hand or inferred, [J] judgment. Effort: S < 3 days, M = 1–3 weeks, L > 3 weeks. Horizons: H1 = before or during the Q3'26 season (reports land late November), H2 = this quarter, H3 = within a year.

## The thesis in one paragraph

Today fox *shows*. Something special *tells, tests and travels*: it tells you what changed before you open the PDF (notes, alerts), lets you test what would happen (stress, previews), and becomes the source others build on (API, Excel link, embeds). All three run on assets nobody else has: the vintage fact table, 248 IFRS 17 sensitivity rows for 7 insurers (FY'25: rates 56, equity 56, mortality 68, morbidity 68) and 75 solvency-ratio sensitivities since FY'20 [V `csm/sens.json`, `csm/solvency.json`], the autopilot (dry run 23.10: 305 documents seen, LLM and push off [V `autopilot/status.json`]), the MCP server, holdings for 5 managers [V]. **No paid API anywhere:** "AI" means the owner's existing Claude subscription driving the MCP at pipeline time, plus deterministic templates; push runs on GitHub Actions + FCM/Telegram, free.

Fork to settle first: PRODUCT.md names one user, the owner; nine of the fourteen directions assume outside users. Recommendation [J]: stay pro-first, treat outside reach as distribution, and keep any consumer tool a separate sub-brand.

## H1 · next month

### D1 · Report-day note writer, "the analyst that has read the filing" (M)
**User & job:** sell-side and buy-side insurer analysts, and the owner, who need a ten-bullet "what changed" note within hours of a filing. **Only fox:** the note is built from the vintage fact table, so every bullet carries a page link and a restated-vs-original flag. No free or paid venue writes IFRS 17 notes for Israeli insurers. **Free data:** MAYA PDFs + XBRL (already in the pipeline), `targets.json` and `adjusted.json` (company targets and adjusted KPIs as printed) [V]. **MVP:** templated sentences from ReportDay deltas (headline, CSM movement, NB vs release, solvency vs target, targets vs actual, restatements); an optional prose pass in Claude Code over the MCP; a validator rejects any digit that does not match a fact id. **Risks:** a misstated number under the fox name: validator, an "אוטומטי · נבדק מול N עובדות" stamp, and owner approval of each note in the first season. **Proof:** Q3'26 notes for 7 insurers with 0 unmatched digits, ≤ 20% of bullets edited by the owner, and the note live ≤ 3 h after the filing.

### D2 · Watch & alert engine (M)
**User & job:** anyone who follows the sector without opening the site daily: "tell me when Harel files, when Clal's solvency drops below target + 10 pts, when Altshuler's net outflows pass ₪1bn in a month." **Only fox:** alerts on *fundamentals* (filing, restatement, CSM, solvency headroom, regulator flows, fee breaks); price alerts are everywhere, these exist nowhere. **Free data:** the autopilot's MAYA poll, data.gov.il CMA monthly datasets (ביטוח-נט, פנסיה-נט, גמל-נט, updated 25.10.26 [V API]), `restatements.json`. **MVP:** autopilot on; a public Telegram channel (free Bot API, no login) and a monthly "regulator data landed" digest; per-user Web Push (FCM) later behind Firebase Auth; GitHub Actions, not Cloud Functions, so the Spark plan holds. **Risks:** false alert on an unverified extraction: send "דווח · טרם נבדק" first, verified deltas only after the gate. **Proof:** median filing-to-alert time ≤ 30 min, 0 retracted numbers, 200 channel subscribers by end of season.

### D3 · Stress & scenario lab (S–M)
**User & job:** buy-side, rating analysts and insurer risk teams: "rates −100 bp and TA-35 −20%: who loses most CSM, equity and solvency headroom, and who can still pay a dividend?" **Only fox:** the disclosed sensitivities of 7 insurers sit in one place, joined to the BoI curve and to the board solvency targets. Today `SensView` shows a static grid [V]. Nobody turns those disclosures into scenarios. **Free data:** `csm/sens.json`, `csm/solvency.json`, BoI SDMX `ZCM` zero-coupon curves (edge.boi.gov.il/FusionEdgeServer/sdmx/v2/…/ZCM) [V-prior sources.md], board targets and surplus from `/industry/capital`. **MVP:** sliders for rates, equities, lapse; effect on equity, CSM and solvency ratio interpolated between disclosed points; historical presets from actual BoI/CBS moves (2022 rates, March '20, October '23); output a ranked bar of headroom over board target and dividend capacity. **Risks:** extrapolation, summing shocks with no diversification, company-specific shock definitions: clamp to the disclosed range, tag "אקסטרפולציה" and "ללא פיזור". **Proof:** reproduces 100% of disclosed values at disclosed sizes; shared-scenario URLs as usage.

### D4 · fox Live Link: open API, Excel/Sheets connector, citable snapshots (S)
**User & job:** the analyst whose model lives in Excel and wants inputs that refresh with a page reference, and academics who need a citable dataset. **Only fox:** a model input that carries its filing page. Koyfin and TIKR gate exports, and Fiscal.ai sells auditability only on Max [V-prior round 1]. **Free data:** the existing static JSON (44 data families [V]), plus Zenodo (free DOIs per quarterly snapshot) and GitHub + jsDelivr as a free CDN so heavy pulls never hit Firebase's free transfer cap [T]. **MVP:** versioned `/api/v1/` CSV/JSON per company × line × period with `doc_id,page`; a Power Query / IMPORTDATA recipe; CC BY 4.0; quarterly Zenodo DOI; `lib/cite.ts` exists [V]. **Risks:** misquotation (as-of and page on every row), schema churn (keep v1 a year). **Proof:** ≥ 10 external workbooks or scripts pulling weekly (count distinct referrers), first academic or press citation of the DOI.

## H2 · this quarter

### D5 · Preview & nowcast engine, with a scorecard (M)
**User & job:** analysts before a report: "what should Q3'26 look like?" Fox has no consensus to offer, but it can compute expectations from public inputs. **Only fox:** the Savings Lens already nowcasts fund-house fee revenue [V `lib/lens.ts`]. Extend it to the insurers' biggest swing factor: *variable management fees* on pre-2004 profit-sharing policies (15% of real return, lost until drawdowns are recovered [T]). ביטוח-נט publishes those policies' monthly returns, and fox already ingests them, including the 1990–2003 step [V RESUME]. Add a financial-margin proxy from the D3 sensitivities. **Free data:** ביטוח-נט and גמל-נט monthly, BoI curve, CBS CPI (api.cbs.gov.il) [V-prior]. **MVP:** per insurer, Q-to-date variable fees and investment-margin direction labelled אומדן with inputs; a preview-vs-actual scorecard after the filing. **Risks:** reads like guidance: publish a 12-quarter backtest, no per-share numbers, no "beat/miss". **Proof:** backtest MAPE on variable fees ≤ 25% and the right QoQ sign in ≥ 75% of quarters, published on the page.

### D6 · Regulatory & policy tracker with event markers on every chart (M)
**User & job:** strategy and compliance teams, journalists, the buy-side: "which circulars, drafts and bills move fees, reserves or capital, and when did they hit the numbers?" **Only fox:** the regulatory event is pinned to the affected series (fee-yield chart, solvency, CSM) as a marker. InsurTool and prodata have no regulatory layer [V-prior]. **Free data:** Knesset OData V4 (`knesset.gov.il/OdataV4/ParliamentInfo/KNS_Bill`, committee sessions and documents; 200 today [V]); CMA circulars, notices and drafts on gov.il (PDFs under `gov.il/BlobFolder/dynamiccollectorresultitem/…`; 403 to curl, so a polite low-volume headless read [V-prior]); tazkirim.gov.il for draft-law memoranda [T]. **MVP:** keyword-tagged feed (ביטוח, פנסיה, גמל, כושר פירעון, דמי ניהול) by line and company; per-company timeline; chart markers. **Risks:** reads as legal interpretation (title, date, status, link only); gov.il blocking (cache, low volume). **Proof:** ≥ 90% of CMA circulars tagged within 48 h, and markers on ≥ 5 chart families.

### D7 · CFO-office peer pack (M)
**User & job:** finance, IR and strategy teams of the 17 groups, who rebuild a peer deck every quarter by hand. **Only fox:** a series (not a single quarter like InsurTool [V-prior]) with page cites, exported as native PPTX through the existing chart book. **Free data:** everything already in the fact table, SOP and Savings Lens. **MVP:** 12 slides per company (share by line, CSM growth, NB/release, expense and combined ratio, solvency headroom, fee yield, cost/income), each with quartile vs peer median and a comparability-footnote field. **Risks:** disputes from the companies compared; footnotes and page links are the defence. **Proof:** pack downloads per season, and the first inbound request from an insurer finance team.

### D8 · Journalist kit: embeddable cards and the "chart of the week" (S–M)
**User & job:** the ≈10–20 reporters on the insurance and pension beat [J round 1], who need a correct, sourced chart by tonight. **Only fox:** an embed that carries its source page and as-of date and updates itself, with no paywall and no logo tax. **Free data:** existing series. **MVP:** iframe/oEmbed card per chart with a source footer, SVG/PNG print fallback, and an auto-picked weekly "chart of the week" from the largest regulator or filing move. **Risks:** bandwidth (D4 CDN); misreadings (the footer cannot be removed). **Proof:** ≥ 5 embeds or credited charts in Calcalist, Globes, TheMarker or Funder within a quarter, and referral traffic from them.

### D9 · Manager due-diligence dossier (M–L)
**User & job:** pension committees, employer consultants and family offices choosing or reviewing a manager. **Only fox:** flows (regulator) × P&L (filings) × holdings (asset lists) in one dossier. prodata has flows without P&L or holdings in its agent tool [V round 1]; ProData Capital sells holdings behind Tableau [V round 1]. **Free data:** gemel/pension/insurance-net monthly; quarterly asset lists on manager sites (5 done in `/holdings` [V]; data.gov.il copies stale since 21.3.2023 [V API]); annual expected investment policy per track on manager sites [T]. **MVP:** one URL per manager: AUM and flows, fee yield, margin, mix vs declared policy, illiquid share, concentration, peer overlap, crisis drawdowns; PDF export. **Risks:** reads as a rating (components only, no score); site format drift. **Proof:** all 9 fund houses + 5 insurer LTS arms covered, and dossier exports per month.

### D10 · M&A and ownership intelligence (L)
**User & job:** bankers, buy-side and the owner's own valuation work: "what has a ₪bn of gemel AUM changed hands for, and who controls whom?" **Only fox:** a precedent table for Israeli insurance and LTS deals (price per ₪bn AUM, implied P/B from filed financials) linked to the cross-holdings graph. **Free data:** MAYA immediate reports (acquisitions, control changes), annual-report notes on business combinations (purchase price and net assets, already in the PDF corpus and searchable in the 108k-page index [V round-2 brief]), CMA control-permit notices on gov.il [T], `/ownership` (Harel only; MAYA blocks automation [V]). **MVP:** curated deal log from FY'19 via full-text search (רכישה, צירוף עסקים, היתר שליטה), each with source page, P/AUM and P/B; then "precedents" on `/valuation`. **Risks:** curation load; earn-outs distort multiples (flag "תמורה מותנית"). **Proof:** ≥ 15 sourced deals and the precedent range shown beside SOTP.

## H3 · within a year

### D11 · Savings transparency tool for the public (M, separate sub-brand)
**User & job:** a saver asking "is my track expensive and how has it really done versus its category?" **Only fox:** net-of-fee track history joined to *the manager's own economics* (what share of the fee is margin), which neither the regulator's tools nor FUNDER show [J]. **Free data:** גמל-נט, פנסיה-נט, ביטוח-נט, `transfer_bank` (fund ids for transfers) [V API]. **MVP:** look up a fund id: fee and 1/3/5/10-year returns vs category, crisis drawdowns, a generic fee-drag calculator. **Risks: legal first.** Personalised pension or investment advice needs a licence (Supervision of Financial Services (Advice, Marketing and Pension Clearing) Law 2005; investment advice law 1995): so no "right for me", no questionnaire, no ranking, a "השוואה, לא המלצה" banner. Also brand dilution and support load; own route and theme. **Proof:** return visits, share-card usage, and zero regulator or complaint contact.

### D12 · IFRS 17 academy on real numbers (M)
**User & job:** CPA and CFA students, juniors at banks and insurers, journalists new to the beat. **Only fox:** worked examples on live data (the Harel Q2'26 CSM roll-forward line by line, the IFRS 4↔17 bridge), each number clickable to its page. **Free data:** the fact table, `taxonomy/lines.json` labels [V]. **MVP:** `/learn`, outside the terminal so "no prose in the UI" holds: 12 lessons, a taxonomy glossary, "explain this line" deep links. **Risks:** technical errors (owner review); prose creeping into the product. **Proof:** traffic from university domains, and lesson completion.

### D13 · Climate and concentration from holdings (L)
**User & job:** allocators, regulators and ESG committees: "how much fossil, real estate or single-issuer concentration does each manager carry, and is it changing?" **Only fox:** exposure measured from the *actual* security-level holdings over time, not from self-reported ESG prose. **Free data:** quarterly asset lists (D9), the TASE sector classification for local issuers [T], OpenFIGI (free open symbology API) for foreign ISINs [T], and company ESG reports on MAYA and IR sites for the disclosed side. **MVP:** sector, real-estate and illiquid exposure per manager over 8 quarters; top 20 issuers held across all 17 groups. **Risks:** misclassification, implied greenwashing accusations: publish the mapping. **Proof:** coverage of ≥ 90% of AUM by mapped issuer.

### D14 · Verified-professional annotations (M)
**User & job:** analysts marking one-offs ("שחרור עתודה חד-פעמי", "שינוי מתודולוגיה") on a figure, so the next reader sees context. **Only fox:** annotations anchored to a fact key with vintage, so a note survives restatements. The existing notes-on-figures plus sync are the base [V round-2 brief]. **Free data:** none new. **MVP:** allowlisted contributors (Google sign-in), public read, a source link per note. **Risks:** defamation and securities-law exposure on listed issuers, moderation load: invite-only, no anonymity, a takedown path. **Proof:** ≥ 1 sourced annotation per filing from people other than the owner.

## Recommended top 5

1. **D1 Note writer:** turns the graphing platform into an analyst for the Q3'26 season; reuses autopilot, fact table, MCP.
2. **D2 Alerts:** the habit loop; fundamentals-only, unique, free to run.
3. **D3 Stress lab:** data already extracted; a small step to something no one has.
4. **D5 Preview engine:** proprietary insight from public data; the published backtest makes trust visible.
5. **D4 Live Link:** cheapest path to source-of-record; every model built on it carries fox's page links.

Sequencing: D3, D4 first (S, no dependencies); D2 needs the autopilot on (owner budget approval); D1 rides D2's pipeline into late November; D5 reuses D3's interpolation.
