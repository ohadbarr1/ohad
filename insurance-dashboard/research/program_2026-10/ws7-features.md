# ws7 · Features and analyst workflows: what a top-tier fundamental terminal offers, what fox has, what to build on free data

Evidence base (22 Oct 2026): live DOM dumps and screenshots in `shots/ws7_*` (routes named below), source under `web/src/`, data under `web/public/data/` and `data/registry/`, the teardowns in `research/*.md`, and vendor pages fetched today. Tags: **[V]** I verified it myself today (live fox DOM/source, or the vendor's own page); **[T]** second-hand (prior teardown that was logged in, review sites, help-centre snippets); **[J]** judgment. Finchat rebranded to Fiscal.ai (one column). prodata.co.il is reachable only as a marketing page (`go.prodata.co.il`) plus the app's shell; the app is behind a personal access code and I did not go further. EY InsurTool is the public Power BI catalogued in `research/insurtool_catalog.csv`. Effort: S < 1 day, M = 2–5 days, L > 1 week.

---

## 1. Verdict

fox's **feature layer scores 5.5/10** today: a tier-1 fact base (full statements for 13 issuers, CSM bridges, solvency rows, 1,251 regulator tracks, a source page on every number) sitting under a thin workflow layer. The things an analyst does *between* reads are mostly missing: nothing tells you a filing landed (the "חדש" chip only works if you happen to open the home page), nothing syncs across devices (every preference, note, watchlist and saved view is a `localStorage` key, `lib/local.ts` [V]), the only export is per-chart CSV/PNG with no source stamp, there is no formula column, no peer-set object, no calendar, no print view, no English, and the "screener" is a 19-column matrix plus a 29-line comparison page, not the 5,077 statement lines. The best general competitor on workflow is **Koyfin at 8.5 [V]** (command bar with ticker+function codes, saved templates with custom hotkeys, dashboards, sharing, 5,900-filter screener, custom formulas at $79/mo); **Fiscal.ai 8 [T]** (custom metrics, cross-document query, earnings calendar, copilot with click-through to filing, all paid). On *this* niche no competitor is above 3: Fiscal.ai shows Harel on a generic template with six free quarters and "no investor relations events" [T], Koyfin shows "Gross Profit, EBITDA, Cash 75bn" for Harel [T], prodata is a fund-selection tool for licensed agents with no statements at all [V], EY InsurTool is a single-quarter cross-section with no time series [V catalog]. fox already wins on data; the engagement is about turning the fact base into an analyst's daily instrument, and the three moves that do that are a report-day autopilot (poll → extract → diff → push), a peer-table builder with formula columns over the whole taxonomy, and provenance-carrying Excel/PowerPoint export.

### 2.1 Findings (each with a one-paragraph spec)

**W7-1. Nothing tells the analyst a filing landed; the pipeline is hand-run.** Evidence: no scheduler, no function, no notification code anywhere in `web/src` or `pipeline/` (`fetch_maya.py` + `deploy.sh` are run by the owner, RESUME "פריסה: רק דרך ./deploy.sh") [V]; home "חדש" chip depends on visiting the home page and `seen` is overwritten on leaving it (`Home.tsx` L25–27) [V]. Why it matters: report day is *the* analyst moment; Harel Q2'26 was filed 26.08.2026 at a known hour and the page should be there before the sell-side note. Spec — *user story:* "when any of my 17 issuers files on MAYA I get a push/email within 15 minutes with the headline deltas and a link to the report-day page." *Data (free):* MAYA's in-page API (`/api/v1/reports/companies`, same-origin only, 403 to curl — `research/sources.md`) polled by a headless Chrome job; the PDF pattern `mayafiles.tase.co.il/rpdf/<range>/P<id>-00.pdf` downloads cleanly with curl [T sources.md]; delivery via Firebase Cloud Messaging (push) and a free-tier mail API. *UI:* a bell in the top bar with an inbox ("הראל · Q2'26 · דוח רבעוני · 180 עמ׳ · לפני 12 דק׳"), per-company toggle on the star, and the report-day page showing "טרם חולץ" cards until extraction finishes. *Effort:* L (M for poll+notify, L with auto-extraction). *Dependency:* ws6 (functions, auth), W7-25.

## 3. Radical moves

**R1. Report-Day Autopilot.** What: a scheduled job polls MAYA for the 17 issuers, downloads the PDF the minute it is filed, runs the existing extraction spec (v6 statements, IFRS 17, solvency) under a token budget, runs `check_fs.py`/`check_workbook.py`, deploys, and pushes "הראל Q2'26: רווח נקי 1,068 (+28% YoY), 30 ערכים הוצגו מחדש" to the phone with the report-day page behind it; until checks pass the page shows the PDF, the calendar status and an "unverified" badge on extracted cards. Why it beats the field: nobody does report-day for Israeli insurers at all (Fiscal.ai: six generic quarters, no events for Harel [T]; Koyfin: no IFRS 17 lines [T]; EY: quarterly PowerPoint-style refresh weeks later [V]); the owner currently *is* the pipeline. Cost: M for poll+push, L for auto-extraction (per report roughly the Sonnet budget RESUME records per document; cap it and queue the rest), ws6 backend. Risks: MAYA's in-page API needs headless Chrome (curl is 403) and may rate-limit; day-one extraction errors reach the screen — mitigated by the "unverified until checks pass" state and by never overwriting a verified period.

**R2. Peer Table Builder (the screener that fits a 17-company universe).** What: one page replaces `/industry/matrix`, `/industry/lines`, `/industry/headline` and `/compare`: any metric from a unified registry (statement roles, IFRS 17 facts, SOP lines, LTS metrics, regulator metrics) × any saved peer set × Q/H/FY/LTM × value/YoY, formula columns, footnote markers per cell, templates with hotkeys, xlsx with page links. Why: Koyfin's 5,900 filters and Fiscal.ai's custom metrics are paid and generic [V]/[T]; fox's version is free, insurer-native and every cell opens its page. Cost: M–L across W7-2/3/4/16. Risks: registry work exposes inconsistent bases (gross/net, group/insurer) — keep the ¹–⁷ footnote system mandatory on every derived cell; mobile layout must collapse to a ranked list (ws3).

**R3. Provenance-native export: Excel backend + IB chart book.** What: every table → xlsx with real numbers, unit rows, `Q2'26` headers, per-cell hyperlinks to the PDF page and a sources sheet; every chart → stamped PNG; a book → PPTX with takeaway titles and source footnotes; "הורד את חוברת החברה" downloads the whole company. Why: Fiscal.ai sells "Data Auditability To Filing" and "Export & Download" only on Max [T]; Koyfin has no Excel add-in [T]; prodata brands PDFs for agents [V]; nobody exports *with the source trail*. It also converts fox from a screen into the owner's model input. Cost: M (W7-12/13/14). Risks: Hebrew/RTL in xlsx and pptx needs testing in Excel for Mac and PowerPoint; file sizes for the full workbook.

**R4. CSM Ledger: the franchise view of the sector.** What: a cross-company IFRS 17 economics page and company tab with time series of new-business CSM, release, interest accretion, estimate changes, loss component, CSM/equity, CSM growth ratio (NB/release, EY's definition [V catalog]), runoff schedule overlay, and — on the valuation page only — P/(Equity+CSM) with price as the labelled input; segment split life/health with the basis chip.
