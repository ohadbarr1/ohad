# ws2 · Desktop UX / IA audit of fox (22 Oct 2026)

Method: every route rendered headless at 1440×900 (36 routes) and 1920×1080 (5 routes), DOM dumps of all 36 routes scanned by script (`ws2_scan.py`: controls before the first table, ISO dates, period formats, Latin prose, buttons without names, aria attributes, `<title>`), source read in `web/src` where a control's behaviour mattered. Screenshots: `shots/ws2_*.png`; DOMs: `dom/ws2_*.html`. Evidence tags: [V] verified here, [T] second-hand (internal teardowns in `research/`, vendor pages), [J] judgment. Effort: S < ½ day, M 1–3 days, L > 3 days.

## 1. Verdict

Desktop fox today: **6.5 / 10**. The data layer and the "Report Day" page are top-tier; the information architecture around them is not. Audit-3's correctness items are largely fixed (per-share units, SOP rebuild, fund-house tabs, density toggle, capital-tab date chips, valuation verdict quarantined, `fin.std` per company), but almost none of the IA items moved: the company page still opens on a 3,400 px card wall with no lead, the statements page still puts 42 controls above the first number, there are still three sub-navigations and three page titles for one regulator dataset, five period formats live side by side, and a shared link reads "fox" in every tab and silently lands on the home page when it is wrong. Best comparable desktop experience: Koyfin **8.5** [T] (ticker + function, one fixed tree per company, templates, permissioned sharing) and Fiscal.ai's statements screen **8** [T] (one control row, chart docked above a checkbox table, every state a URL). fox already has the URL state and the per-cell provenance that neither ships at base tier; what it lacks is hierarchy: one thing leading each screen, one vocabulary, and one path per analyst job.

## 3. Radical moves

**RM-1 · Company page: Report Day becomes the company home; eight tabs become four.** New tabs: דוח (Report Day with the review's segment rows merged in) · דוחות (statements; SOP and IFRS 17 become sheet groups in the existing sheet picker, with their own layouts) · חיסכון · מסמכים. The 19-card grid survives as "פרופיל" below the Report Day table. Why it beats Koyfin: Koyfin's Overview is quote-led and generic; Fiscal's is description + statistics; neither opens on "what changed vs. last year and last quarter, with every number on its page" [T]. Cost: M (routing, context lift of period/window, card grid move). Risk: the owner likes the card grid as a first impression — keep it one scroll down; fund houses need the Report Day headline roles to resolve (they do today, `ws2_alt_report_1440.png`).

**RM-2 · One "Series" engine replaces four pages.** `/industry/lines`, `/industry/headline`, the matrix's "לאורך זמן" panel and the statements chart become one page: measure picker spanning XBRL KPIs, taxonomy-mapped statement lines (the 5,077-line map in `lib/labels.ts`), IFRS 17 facts and regulator metrics; companies as chips; Q/H/FY/LTM; standard-break band; templates ("CSM economics", "fee yield") saved and applied to any company; every state a URL; PNG stamped with source. Why it beats Koyfin: Koyfin's multi-ticker graphs and templates [T] have no insurance measures and no Israeli segment data; fox would have the measures and the page links. Cost: L (taxonomy aliases across IFRS 4/17, the engine itself is mostly `foxOption` + `IndustryLines` logic). Risk: alias quality; mitigate by shipping with the 40 lines that already have roles in `lines.json`.

**RM-3 · Collapse the regulator section to one layout and kill `/companies`.** `/market/*` folds into `/funds` (tabs מסלולים · יצרן מול השוק · שוק · מנהלים; ranking becomes the שוק lead chart); `/companies` folds into the home table with a column-set switch. One rail item = one subnav = one h1. Cost: S–M (routes redirect; `MarketOverview`/`MarketRanking` keep their components). Risk: existing shared links — keep `Navigate` redirects for a year.

**RM-4 · Industry hub as a league table, not a tab bar.** Matrix first (it is already sortable, footnoted and sourced), with column sets instead of tabs (רווחיות · CSM · הון ודיבידנד · פנסיה וגמל · נכסים), the sectors deck as the lead chart for the selected column set, and "סדרות" (RM-2) and "חיפוש" as the only other tabs. Nine tabs → three. Why: Koyfin's Compare Matrix is in beta [T]; fox's is live and better for this sector. Cost: M. Risk: the sectors page's 51 sections are the owner's richest fund-house view — they become the "פנסיה וגמל" column set's detail panel, not deleted.

**RM-5 · Terminal mode at ≥1600 px.** Three panes: statements table (left two-thirds), docked chart (right), source sheet as a side pane instead of a modal; density "צפוף" default on wide screens; container unbounded; the rail stays. The same shell serves Report Day (table | 12-quarter chart | source). Why: the owner works on a 27-inch monitor (PRODUCT.md) and today sees the 1440 layout with margins (`ws2_phoenix_fin_1920.png`). Cost: M (CSS grid + Source pane variant). Risk: none structural; the mobile layout is untouched.

**RM-6 · "שלי": views, watchlist, notes, recents, and real deep links.** Every screen's state in the URL (W2-11/12/28), a page listing saved views across companies and series, followed companies, notes, last 20 routes; anonymous Firebase auth + Firestore for sync (Phase 3, platform dependency). Why: Koyfin's My Graphs folders and permissioned sharing [T] are the one usability feature audit-3 flagged twice; fox can ship the unsynced version in a week. Cost: M (page) + L (sync). Risk: auth is the first server-side state in a static product — keep local-only as the fallback.
