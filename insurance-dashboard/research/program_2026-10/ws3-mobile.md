# ws3 — Mobile UX (fox at phone width)

Method. Live site, 35 distinct routes (all route families in the brief; `/funds/:k` and `/market/group/:name` reached only through their parents), true phone emulation through headless Chrome + CDP (390×844 DPR 2, touch, `hover:none`/`pointer:coarse`, iPhone UA; also 360×780 and 430×932; both themes). Per route I measured document height, document scroll width, position and time of the first figure, controls above the fold and before the first figure, controls under 36 px, `<select>` option counts, sticky elements, table/scroll-container overflow, resource bytes (compressed) and cold time-to-first-figure under slow-4G + 4× CPU throttling. Raw numbers: `shots/ws3_{a,b,c,d,dk,360,430,thr*}_metrics.json`; harness `ws3_mobile.mjs`, `ws3_clip.mjs`; screenshots `shots/ws3_*.png` (all under the scratchpad). Source lines refer to `web/src/`. Effort: S < half a day, M = 1–3 days, L > 3 days. Tags: [V] verified on the vendor's page/app listing or on fox, [T] second-hand, [J] judgment.

## 1. Verdict

fox on a phone is a shrunk desktop with one genuinely mobile-first page (`/company/:id/report`) and one live regression that touches 24 of 35 routes: a phone-only CSS rule meant for the review table turns every `.panel .scroll` into `overflow: visible`, so the document is 453–1,657 px wide at 390 px, the page pans sideways, and digits, minus signs and dates are cut at the left edge on home, companies, matrix, capital, headline, IFRS 17, econ, market and managers. The review tab's sign-clipping (A3-A7) is fixed, but its replacement glues the change to the comparative ("−5.5%957", "−9235.0"). Statements still open on 43 controls before the first number (743 px, below the 709 px usable fold — worse than audit 3's 677). Nothing exists above the page level: no manifest, no service worker, no push, no sync, no system-dark, an SVG touch icon iOS cannot use. Score today: **5/10** (audit 3 said 6; the overflow regression and the untouched platform layer cost a point). Best competitor on a phone: **StockUnlock 8 [V]** (free native iOS/Android apps, earnings and movers push alerts, web↔app sync) and **Koyfin mobile 7.5 [V]** (compact app, iOS widgets, alerts); TIKR has no app and says so [T, official support page]; prodata.co.il is a login-walled agent tool with no mobile claim at all [V]. fox's lead is that none of them has an IFRS 17 report card, a per-number source sheet, or a report-day diff on a phone; the gap is that fox has not yet decided what the phone product is.

Visible damage: home "דיווחים אחרונים" dates cut to "026-08-27" (`shots/ws3_a_home.png`), companies YoY "107.9%" cut to "07.9%" (`ws3_a_companies.png`), capital "30%", "44% · 139%" half digits (`ws3_c_industry_capital.png`), matrix and IFRS 17 tables run off the left edge (`ws3_b_industry_matrix.png`, `ws3_a_company_phoenix_ifrs17.png`); the sticky header shifts with the pan (`ws3_sc_iifrs_y1100.png`). ## 3. Radical moves

**R1. Make "report day" the phone home: a feed, not a dashboard.** What: `/` at ≤ 760 px becomes a reverse-chronological feed of filings from the MAYA registry; each item is the four-figure report card (insurance revenue, service result, net profit, CSM/equity; YoY · QoQ · restated count) with one tap to the full `/report`; followed companies pinned; a quiet row for non-followed. Push (R4) lands on the same card. Why it beats the named competitors: StockUnlock's and Koyfin's mobile surfaces are price-first watchlists [V]; TIKR has no app [T]; prodata has no phone story [V]; nobody can render an IFRS 17 report card because nobody has the extraction. Cost: M (data and the `/report` components exist; registry freshness is the dependency, ws1/ws2 data stream). Risks: registry lag makes the feed stale; the feed must degrade to "latest per company" when nothing is new.

**R2. Replace the eight-tab company page with a "company card" on phone.** What: one scrolling card — 44 px header, six KPI tiles (2-up, sparkline-only), the report-day block, CSM bridge as a 5-bar horizontal waterfall, solvency + dividend capacity, last three filings; statements, SOP, IFRS 17 and filings open as full-screen sheets launched from the tile they belong to ("CSM → תנועה ב-CSM"). Why: Qualtrim's grid of one-metric cards is the recognised phone bar [T, `research/qualtrim_teardown.md`]; fox adds a source page behind every tile and a sector no one else has. Cost: M (layout switch over existing components; desktop untouched). Risk: two layouts to maintain — mitigated by driving both from one `cards` spec (`CompanyOverview.tsx` already builds card specs).

**R3. A statement "ledger" for thumbs.** What: at ≤ 760 px the statements table becomes a ledger: label on line one, latest figure right-aligned, YoY/QoQ chips on line two; tap a row → bottom sheet with the full period run as a horizontal bar chart, restated marks and the source page; sheet/period/standard in one sticky 44 px context bar (W3-2) with bottom-sheet pickers; "כל התקופות" stays as a landscape mode. Why: Fiscal.ai and TIKR render the desktop table on a phone [T]; a ledger is how banking apps already taught every reader to scan 40 lines. Cost: M. Risk: losing the cross-period scan — covered by the row sheet and landscape.

**R4. Install + notify + sync (the platform layer).** What: manifest, service worker (shell precache, data SWR, offline last-viewed companies), Web Push/FCM on new filing and on restatement, Firebase auth for watchlist/notes/saved views (A3 6c-6). Why: it is the difference between a bookmark and a product; StockUnlock's free-tier alerts and web↔app sync are its whole mobile pitch [V]; Koyfin's widgets ship "on all plans" [V]. Cost: M (two weeks incl. iOS standalone push caveat). Risk: iOS push only for installed PWAs — the install prompt (R1 banner after the second visit) is part of the move.

**R5. Two primitives before any page: `Sheet` and `ContextBar`.** What: a bottom sheet (drag, back-button aware, search, grouped options, recent) and a sticky context bar (shows the chosen report/period/basis, opens the sheet); every `select`, seg strip, range slider and the source panel migrate to them. Why: it removes the control walls on all 14 routes that have them at once and gives the product one phone gesture vocabulary; the source sheet already proved the pattern. Cost: S–M. Risk: none beyond migration discipline.

**R6. Cut on phone.** `/market/*` duplicates of `/funds/*` (one subnav), `/industry/search` as a tab (fold into the header search), `/valuation` from the bottom nav (keep via company header), the hero mast, the per-card axes, the "כל השורות שדווחו" 281-row tables (CSV instead, as A3 §7). Cost: S. Risk: owner attachment to the mast — keep it on desktop.
